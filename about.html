Our Story

Rooted in
Stewardship

With roots reaching back to 1927, our story is one of partnership and respect for nature.

Heritage

Nearly a century of protection

This land has been protected for generations, guided by the Shangaan principle of Ntumbuluko — a deep harmony with the land that shapes everything we do.

Today, under shared community ownership, Tengile MalaMala continues to protect its wilderness and empower its people, entering a new chapter of focused stewardship and long-term investment.
